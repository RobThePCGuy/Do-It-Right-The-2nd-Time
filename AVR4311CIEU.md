
### **Equipment and Configuration Overview**

**Receiver:** Denon AVR-4311CI (9.2 channels with 11.2 processing capability when using an external amplifier)

**External Amplifier:** To power two of the front speakers

**Speakers (10 Total):**

1. **Front Speakers (4 Total):**
   - Front Left (FL) — powered by external amplifier
   - Front Right (FR) — powered by external amplifier
   - Front Wide Left (FWL)
   - Front Wide Right (FWR)

2. **Side Speakers (2 Total):**
   - Surround Left (SL)
   - Surround Right (SR)

3. **Ceiling Speakers (2 Total):**
   - Front Height Left (FHL)
   - Front Height Right (FHR)

4. **Rear Speakers (2 Total):**
   - Surround Back Left (SBL)
   - Surround Back Right (SBR)

**Subwoofers (Optional):** Up to 2 subwoofers can be connected.

---

### **Step-by-Step Setup Instructions**

#### **Step 1: Connect the External Amplifier**

1. **Power Off All Equipment:**
   - Ensure the receiver, external amplifier, and all source devices are turned off and unplugged from the power outlet.

2. **Locate Pre-Out Connections on the Receiver:**
   - On the back of the Denon AVR-4311CI, find the **"Pre Out"** section.
   - Identify the **Front Left (FL)** and **Front Right (FR)** pre-out jacks.

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 224413](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fb8fbc7-f45c-4ce4-a8c6-a022f0762616)

3. **Connect Receiver to External Amplifier:**
   - Use high-quality RCA audio cables to connect the **FL pre-out** to the **left channel input** of your external amplifier.
   - Connect the **FR pre-out** to the **right channel input** of your external amplifier.

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 225124](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/11beb3ad-f85a-44e6-b1ff-6666d71acdbf)

4. **Connect Front Speakers to External Amplifier:**
   - Connect your **Front Left speaker** to the **left speaker output** of the external amplifier.
   - Connect your **Front Right speaker** to the **right speaker output** of the external amplifier.

#### **Step 2: Connect Remaining Speakers to the Receiver**

1. **Front Wide Speakers:**
   - Connect **Front Wide Left (FWL)** speaker to the receiver’s **Front Wide Left** speaker terminal.
   - Connect **Front Wide Right (FWR)** speaker to the receiver’s **Front Wide Right** speaker terminal.

   - Connect **Surround Right (SR)** speaker to the receiver’s **Surround Right** speaker terminal.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/51730fe1-c391-4308-8ccd-c7c20f03e1e9)

3. **Surround Back Speakers:**
   - Connect **Surround Back Left (SBL)** speaker to the receiver’s **Surround Back Left** speaker terminal.
   - Connect **Surround Back Right (SBR)** speaker to the receiver’s **Surround Back Right** speaker terminal.

4. **Front Height (Ceiling) Speakers:**
   - Connect **Front Height Left (FHL)** speaker to the receiver’s **Front Height Left** speaker terminal.

#### **Step 3: Power On and Initial Setup**

1. **Plug In and Power On:**
   - Plug in the receiver and external amplifier.
   - Turn on the receiver, external amplifier, and your display device (TV or monitor).

2. **Select the Correct Input on Your Display Device:**
   - Ensure your TV or monitor is set to display the receiver's on-screen menu.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0e21c80-b44d-4c48-9835-b075933aa42f)

#### **Step 4: Receiver Configuration**

**A. Access the Setup Menu:**

1. **Using the Remote Control:**
   - Press the **"Setup"** button to access the receiver's setup menu.

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9051244a-0dde-4bc8-9368-5b52d331fb69)

**B. Navigate to Amp Assign Settings:**

1. **Select "Manual Setup":**
   - Use the arrow keys to highlight **"Manual Setup"** and press **"Enter"**.

2. **Select "Amp Assign":**
   - Navigate to **"Amp Assign"** and press **"Enter"**.

**C. Configure Amp Assign Settings:**

1. **Set Assign Mode to "11.2ch":**
   - Highlight **"Assign Mode"** and select **"11.2ch"** to enable 11-channel processing.

2. **Set Power Amp Assign:**
   - Choose **"Front Height + Front Wide"** to assign internal amplifiers to both Front Height and Front Wide speakers.

3. **Set Front Speaker to "PreAmp":**
   - Under **"Front Speaker"**, select **"PreAmp"** to indicate that the Front Left and Front Right speakers are powered by an external amplifier.

**D. Save Settings:**
   - Press the **"Return"** or **"Back"** button to save settings and exit to the previous menu.

#### **Step 5: Speaker Configuration**

1. **Select "Speaker Configuration":**
   - From the **"Manual Setup"** menu, choose **"Speaker Configuration"** and press **"Enter"**.

2. **Set Speaker Presence and Size:**

   - **Front Speakers:** Set to **"Large"** or **"Small"** based on your speakers' bass capabilities.
   - **Center Speaker:** Set to **"None"** (since you haven't included one).
   - **Subwoofer:** Set to **"Yes"** if using subwoofers.
   - **Front Height Speakers:** Set to **"Yes"**.
   - **Front Wide Speakers:** Set to **"Yes"**.
   - **Surround Speakers:** Set to **"Yes"**.
   - **Surround Back Speakers:** Set to **"2 spkrs"**.

3. **Set Crossover Frequencies:**
   - Adjust the crossover settings for each speaker group according to the manufacturer's specifications (commonly set to 80Hz as a starting point).

4. **Set Bass Settings:**
   - Choose **"LFE+Main"** if you have set any speakers to **"Large"** and are using a subwoofer.
   - Set the **LPF for LFE** to **120Hz**.

#### **Step 6: Distance and Level Calibration**

1. **Select "Distance":**
   - Measure the distance from each speaker to your primary listening position.
   - Input these distances in the **"Distance"** settings.

2. **Select "Channel Level":**
   - Set initial channel levels to **0 dB** for all speakers.

#### **Step 7: Run Audyssey MultEQ XT32 Calibration**

1. **Connect the Calibration Microphone:**
   - Plug the supplied microphone into the **"Setup Mic"** port on the front panel.

2. **Start the Calibration Process:**
   - Navigate back to the main **"Setup"** menu.
   - Select **"Auto Setup"** and then **"Audyssey MultEQ XT32"**.
   - Follow the on-screen instructions.

3. **Position the Microphone:**
   - Place the microphone at ear level in your main listening position.
   - Complete measurements in at least three different positions (up to eight for optimal results).

4. **Save the Calibration Settings:**
   - After measurements are complete, the receiver will calculate the optimal settings.
   - Save these settings when prompted.

#### **Step 8: Verify and Fine-Tune Settings**

1. **Review Speaker Settings:**
   - Go back to **"Speaker Configuration"** and ensure all settings match your physical setup.

2. **Check Channel Levels:**
   - Verify that the Audyssey calibration has set appropriate levels for each speaker.

3. **Adjust as Necessary:**
   - Manually adjust any settings if you feel certain speakers are too loud or too soft.

#### **Step 9: Test the System**

1. **Play Test Tones or Content:**
   - Use the receiver's built-in test tones or play familiar audio content.

2. **Confirm Speaker Operation:**
   - Ensure each speaker is producing sound.
   - Verify that the Front Left and Front Right speakers are functioning through the external amplifier.

3. **Adjust External Amplifier Settings:**
   - If your external amplifier has gain controls, adjust them to match the levels of the other speakers.

#### **Step 10: Select Appropriate Surround Mode**

1. **Choose a Surround Mode that Utilizes All Speakers:**
   - For full utilization of Front Height and Front Wide speakers, select modes like **"Audyssey DSX"** or **"Dolby Pro Logic IIz"**.

2. **Access Surround Mode Settings:**
   - Press the **"Movie"** or **"Music"** button on the remote to cycle through available modes.
